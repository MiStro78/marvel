Spider-Man is a Marvel superhero, best known as Peter Parker, a teenager who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider. His classic powers include: 🕷️ Superhuman strength, agility, and reflexes 🕸️ Web-swinging and web-shooting ⚠️ “Spider-Sense” that warns him of danger 🧗 Ability to climb walls His famous principle is: “With great power comes great responsibility.
